## Getting started
Select a month. Use Calendar for daily plans; Checklist for tasks and notes; Doodle for the four-part drawing studio. Tap a section to open it. Draw with a finger or Apple Pencil, pinch to zoom, and pan with two fingers.

## Saving
Changes save automatically to this device. Keep the app installed. Export monthly PDFs for reference; exported files cannot restore editable data. Device backup settings are managed by Apple.

## Cover photo
Tap the cover on the home screen to edit the title or select a photo. Use an image you own or have permission to use.

## Report a problem
Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send private notes or personal photos unless essential and you choose to do so. If the app reports a corrupt saved file, do not uninstall it before contacting support; uninstalling removes local data.
