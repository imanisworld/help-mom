## Description
Make space for your plans and your imagination with Creative Moments.

Organize your month, capture ideas, and enjoy a seasonal scene to color. This dated 18-month edition brings calendars, checklists, notes, and a four-part doodle studio together in one quiet space.

• Plan from December 2026 through May 2028.
• Add daily plans, monthly intentions, and editable checklists.
• Record inspiration, creative wins, and ideas to carry forward.
• Draw in four spaces: Color the Season, Sketch an Idea, Explore Patterns, and Free Doodle.
• Enjoy twelve realistic seasonal illustrations, coordinated by month.
• Use your finger or Apple Pencil with pen, translucent marker, and eraser tools.
• Personalize your cover with your own photo and title.
• Share a monthly PDF or an individual drawing image.

No account is required. Plans, drawings, and cover photos are stored locally on your device. This edition does not provide cloud sync. Exports are snapshots, not editable backups. Coloring is freehand; there is no tap-to-fill tool.

Created by T. Crumble / Bridging the Gap.

## Review notes
No login or test account is required. Open a month and switch between Calendar, Checklist, and Doodle. Tap a date to enter notes. Tap a doodle section to open the drawing tools. Use the share button to export a month. Cover personalization is on the home screen. Help and privacy are available from the information button.
