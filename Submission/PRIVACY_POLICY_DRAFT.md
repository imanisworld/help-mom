Creative Moments is a personal planner and drawing app. In this version, we do not collect your plans, notes, drawings, photos, identifiers, usage analytics, or location on a developer-operated server. We do not operate user accounts, show ads, or use tracking SDKs.

## Information on your device
Plans, checklists, notes, drawings, and the cover photo you select are saved in the app's local storage. The app uses Apple's system photo picker, which allows you to choose an image without granting broad photo-library access. A copy of the chosen image is stored locally for the cover.

## Sharing and backups
When you choose to export and share a PDF or image, you control the recipient or destination. The selected service handles that file under its own privacy practices. Apple may back up app data according to your device settings; we do not operate that backup service or receive those backups.

## Retention and deletion
Local data remains in the app until you delete it or uninstall the app. Removing a cover photo removes the app's stored copy, not the original in Photos. Apple-managed backups and files you export may remain in their respective destinations until you remove them there. PDF and image exports cannot restore editable app records.

## Support
If you contact the publisher, the publisher receives the information you choose to send. Do not send sensitive planner content unnecessarily. Support correspondence is handled through the contact service you use and is retained only as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

## Changes
Update this policy if the app's data practices change. Add analytics, cloud services, accounts, or other SDKs only after reviewing their privacy implications and updating the policy and App Store disclosures.
